============================================================
1. GAME CONCEPT
============================================================

The game takes place inside a supermarket at night after closing.

Several players play as mannequins.

One player may play as a security guard, OR the guard may be controlled
by AI.

The core mechanic:

MANNEQUINS CAN MOVE WHEN THEY ARE NOT BEING OBSERVED.

WHEN A GUARD LOOKS AT A MANNEQUIN:

GREEN:
Nobody is currently observing the mannequin.
The mannequin can move freely.

ORANGE:
The guard has started looking at the mannequin.
The mannequin has a configurable amount of time to stop moving.

RED:
The mannequin is fully observed.
The mannequin must remain completely still.

If the mannequin moves while RED:

1. suspicion increases by +1
2. continued movement increases suspicion further
3. if the mannequin continues moving after reaching the discovery
   threshold, the mannequin is DISCOVERED.

A discovered player does NOT die.

Instead:

MANNEQUIN
    ↓
CAPTURED
    ↓
WAREHOUSE

Captured players can potentially be rescued.

Captured players may also gain access to the surveillance/security room
and provide information to their teammates.

This means being captured does not necessarily remove the player from
the entire game.

    ↓

    ↓
OBJECTIVES COMPLETED
    ↓
PLAYERS FIND ESCAPE ROUTE
    ↓
PLAYERS ESCAPE
    ↓
WIN

LOSS CONDITIONS:

1. All mannequin players are discovered/captured and no rescue/escape
   condition remains.

2. Dawn occurs before the required escape condition is completed.

The rules must be configurable rather than hardcoded.

============================================================
15. RED STATE
============================================================

RED means:

THE PLAYER IS FULLY OBSERVED.

The player should remain still.

If the player moves:

suspicion += configured amount

Do NOT simply detect keyboard input.

Movement should be measured from actual player movement/state so that
physics/networking cannot accidentally create false positives.

Possible configurable definition:

Movement threshold = X centimeters per second.

Tiny network jitter must not count as movement.
